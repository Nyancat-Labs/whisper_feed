v1.0.0

First release

Whisper is a reader first and a launcher page second. Everything below is new in the sense that it has never been released under this name.

  • Four layouts — Cards, Magazine, List and Mosaic
  • An ordering that explains itself: every article can say why it is the size it is, and any source can be told to appear more or less
  • Breaking-news grouping when several sources carry one story
  • Read state, marked when you scroll past an article rather than while you are reading it
  • Full-text fetching, per source or for everything
  • Search across headlines, sources and summaries
  • A glance row: weather, sunset, and how much is waiting
  • Backup to a folder you choose — subscriptions as OPML any reader can open, and your settings beside it
  • Restore offered on first run, before the starter feeds
  • Optional sync with FreshRSS, Inoreader, Miniflux or BazQux over the Google Reader protocol
  • A guided tour of the feed's controls, replayable from Settings
  • Android’s own backup left off unless you turn it on
